Cookie policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026.

In short. Neither this website nor the Kyros app sets cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no third-party script. That is why you see no cookie banner.

1. This website (kyrosfinance.com)

The website is a set of static files. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The fonts and images are served from the website itself, so no font service or content network is contacted. The only other site your browser talks to is GitHub Pages, which hosts the files (see the privacy policy).

2. The app

The app sets no cookies either. To work, it keeps some data in your browser's local storage: your language, your vaults' addresses, the payments and clients you register, the figures for the tax tools and your testnet keys. The full list is in section 3 of the privacy policy.

That storage is strictly necessary for the service you ask for, so it needs no consent (article 22.2 of Spain's LSSI and article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

3. How to delete it

Clear this site's data in your browser's settings (for example, in Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Site data). If you delete the app's data, keep your wallet's recovery phrase or passkey: your money is in your wallet and your vault on Stellar, not in your browser.

4. Changes

If Kyros ever uses cookies or similar technologies that need your consent, it will ask for it before setting them, and this page will be updated with a new date.
