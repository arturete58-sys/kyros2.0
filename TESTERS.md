# Founding testers

People who tried the Kyros beta on Stellar testnet and helped shape it with
their feedback or their bug reports. Being listed is optional: ask on GitHub
or on X (@ the Kyros account) and you are added, with the name or handle you
choose. Ask the same way to be removed.

Found a bug or a way to break the vault? Open an issue, or for anything that
could put funds at risk, report it privately as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
