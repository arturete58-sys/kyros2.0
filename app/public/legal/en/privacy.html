Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Applies to the Kyros testnet beta.

In short. Kyros has no accounts and no servers of its own, runs no analytics and sets no cookies. We do not receive your personal data. What you enter stays in your browser. To work, your browser talks directly to public Stellar services, to the wallet you choose and, if you pay from another chain, to Circle. Payments on Stellar are public, like any blockchain transaction.

1. Who is responsible

Kyros is a project, not a company. The person responsible for this website and for any personal data handled through it is Arturo Ferrándiz Fernández, co-founder of Kyros. Contact: arturete58@gmail.com.

2. What Kyros is, technically

Kyros is a web app published as static files on GitHub Pages. It runs entirely in your browser. The money logic lives in smart contracts on the Stellar network that each user owns and controls. Kyros has no backend, no user database and no way to see what you do in the app.

3. What stays in your browser

The app keeps the following in your browser's local storage, so it works the next time you open it. It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself:

WhatWhy
Your languageTo show the app in it
Your vaults' addressesTo find your vault again
Expected payments, clients and invoice details you registerTo split those payments by their own terms and show them to you
Figures for the tax tools (expenses, the tax office's notices, last year's tax)To work out your instalments; a PDF notice is read in your browser and is not uploaded
Testnet keysTo sign test transactions; they are only valid on testnet

This storage is strictly necessary for the service you ask for, so it needs no consent banner (article 22.2 of Spain's LSSI and article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

4. Cookies

Kyros sets no cookies: no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party pixels. The fonts ship with the app, so no font service is contacted.

5. Passkeys (Face ID, fingerprint)

If you create a passkey wallet, the passkey is created and kept by your device (and synced by Apple, Google or your password manager if you use one). Your biometric data never leaves your device and Kyros never receives it. The name you give the passkey is stored with it on your device.

6. What is public on the blockchain

Stellar is a public network. Anything done on it can be seen by anyone and cannot be deleted, by Kyros or by anyone else. For Kyros that means: wallet and vault addresses, amounts, dates, the vault's settings, and a hash (a one-way fingerprint) of each invoice reference. Names, invoice contents and client details are not written on-chain. Addresses are pseudonymous, but they can be linked to a person by other means, so do not put personal data in any field that goes on-chain.

8. When you contact us

If you email us, open an issue on GitHub or send feedback, we use what you send (usually your name or username, your email and your message) only to answer you and to improve Kyros. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep it until the matter is closed, and at most 12 months unless you ask us to keep it longer. GitHub issues are public. If we ever ask for feedback through a form, the form will say what it collects, and this policy will be updated.

9. Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, restriction of or objection to its use, and portability, by writing to arturete58@gmail.com. Since Kyros does not hold the data the app uses, there is usually nothing on our side to return or delete: you control it in your browser. Data on the blockchain cannot be erased by anyone. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority where you live.

10. Age

Kyros is meant for adults managing their own taxes. It is not directed at children.

11. Security

How we handle security, and how to report a vulnerability privately, is in our security policy.

12. Changes
